Attentive · always learning
A measured approach

Useful security insight starts with trustworthy evidence.

01 / SCOPE

Monitor authorised assets

Asset ownership and assessment rules are established before monitoring begins. Discovery alone never grants permission to assess.

02 / EVIDENCE

Make observations explainable

Bounded DNS, TLS, web, and email-policy observations feed versioned findings with confidence, limitations, and supporting evidence.

03 / ACTION

Plan the next step

Teams can assign remediation and review risk in context. Acknowledging a finding does not mark it resolved; later evidence does.

Built around clear boundaries

Permission comes first.

Monitoring is limited to assets with verified authority and an explicit policy. No automatic scanning of newly discovered assets.

Findings describe observed evidence and its limits. They are not a certification, penetration test, or guarantee of security.
